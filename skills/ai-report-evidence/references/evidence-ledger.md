# A proportionate evidence record

Use a small table for substantial work. Do not require five registries or a marker on every sentence for an ordinary memo.

| Claim or value | Report location | Source/version and exact locator | Conditions and units | Check status / unresolved issue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One proposition or numeric concept | Section, table, figure, paragraph | File/page/table, record, or code version | Scope needed to interpret the result | Checked against source, unverified, conflicting, or not applicable |

“Checked” means the identified material supports the claim under the recorded conditions. It does not certify the whole study. Record actual human approval separately when it is required.

For repeated numeric facts, also track the concept, denominator, reporting period, rounding, and every repeated location. Keep zero, missing, below detection, and not measured distinct.

A source hash can identify a snapshot; it does not prove truth or anonymize predictable sensitive content. Keep private records in the authorized workspace, using only necessary metadata.

When wording or scope changes, revisit its evidence mapping. A previously valid citation may no longer support the revised claim.
